# Safema 🚨

**Safema** is a women’s safety mobile application integrated with smart wearable devices, designed to provide **real-time protection**, **instant emergency response**, and a **community-driven safety network**. The project combines mobile technology, backend services, and IoT-based wearables to help women feel safe anytime, anywhere.

---

## 🌟 Problem Statement

Women’s safety remains a critical issue worldwide, with many incidents of harassment, assault, and domestic violence going unreported due to fear, lack of evidence, or delayed emergency response. In India alone:

* Over **31,000 rape cases** are reported annually
* **500,000+** cases of domestic violence and related offenses were reported in 2023
* Crimes such as harassment, stalking, chain snatching, and cybercrime continue to rise

Immediate action in such situations can save lives, yet existing safety solutions often fall short.

---

## 💡 Solution

Safema offers a **real-time women’s safety ecosystem** powered by:

* A **mobile app** for tracking, alerts, and reporting
* **Smart wearable devices** (lockets, rings, keychains, belts)
* A **community safety network** of nearby women

With simple press-based actions on a wearable device:

* **1 press** → Share live location via SMS
* **2 presses** → Initiate an emergency call
* **3 presses** → Notify police / SHE Teams

---

## 🔑 Key Features

* 📍 **Live Location Tracking** – Real-time GPS tracking during emergencies
* 🚨 **Instant Alert System** – One-tap emergency alerts via wearables
* 👩‍👩‍👧 **Community Safety Network** – Warn nearby women about suspicious activity
* 🏥 **Safe Space Locator** – Find nearby safe places and public spaces
* 🔔 **Emergency Notifications** – SMS, calls, and alerts sent instantly
* 📢 **Suspicious Activity Reporting** – Report and share alerts with the community

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

### Frontend

* **React Native** – Cross-platform mobile app development
* **Figma** – UI/UX design and prototyping

### Backend

* **Node.js** – Server-side runtime
* **Express.js** – API and server logic
* **MongoDB & Mongoose** – Database and data modeling
* **Firebase** – User data storage and scalability

### Hardware

* **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)** wearable devices
* Custom firmware for SOS triggers, calls, and alerts

---

## ⚙️ System Architecture

* Mobile app connects to wearable via **Bluetooth (BLE)**
* Emergency triggers override app state to send **SMS, calls, and alerts**
* Backend stores user profiles, alert logs, and location data securely
